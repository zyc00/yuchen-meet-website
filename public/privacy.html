Privacy

This is Yuchen Zhou’s personal meeting booking service. It uses the information you submit to arrange your meeting and send a calendar invitation.

Booking information

Your name, email address, meeting topic, selected time, duration, and time zone are stored on Yuchen’s server hosted by Vultr. The information needed for your invitation is sent to Google Calendar and is visible to Yuchen and the invited attendee. The meeting uses a Zoom link; this site does not connect to your Zoom account or create recordings.

Google Calendar access

Only Yuchen connects a Google account. Visitors do not need Google sign-in. The service reads Yuchen’s busy times, creates meeting invitations, and checks changes to bookings it created. Public availability shows occupied time ranges, not private event titles or attendee details. The Google refresh token is encrypted in the server database.

Google account data is used only to provide this booking service. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Yuchen can disconnect Google access through the private administration page or revoke access in Google Account settings.

Storage and retention

Your browser may store a booking reference so you can recover a confirmation after a connection interruption. The service uses hashed rate-limit identifiers to help prevent abuse. This application does not include advertising or analytics trackers. GitHub Pages, Vultr, Google, and Zoom may process technical information when you use their services.

Completed booking records become eligible for routine database cleanup 90 days after the meeting. Unresolved bookings are retained until their status can be reconciled. Calendar events and invitations in Google are not automatically deleted by this cleanup.
